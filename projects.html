Festival della Scienza, quick view

An unofficial app for the Festival della Scienza in Genoa. I built it because the official programme was hard to use quickly: it was difficult to understand what was going on right now, and to keep track of the events I liked. My version shows what is on now and soon, lets you filter events and save your favourites, and works on mobile and desktop.

About 140 people use it, only through word of mouth.

BlaBla community, new website

A redesign of the website for BlaBla, a free, volunteer-run language exchange with events in 70+ cities. The original site was generic, with no link to real events that were happening, so it was hard to tell whether the project was alive or dead.

I made the new one to fix that:

  • faster to load
  • more catchy
  • upcoming events right on the homepage
  • contacts easy to find
