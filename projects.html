Festival della Scienza, quick view

An unofficial app for the Festival della Scienza in Genoa. The official programme was hard to scan quickly: it was difficult to see what was happening now, and to keep track of the events I liked.

  • Time slider: pick a time and see only what is on then, or step half an hour back and forward
  • Map: switch from list to map to see where events are and what is close to you
  • Favourites: mark events as going or maybe, and show only those
  • Personal plan: your saved events in one place
  • Search: by title, speaker, description or place
  • Italian and English

It spread by word of mouth and is used by about 140 people. Open the app.

BlaBla community, new website

A new website for BlaBla, a free, volunteer-run language exchange with events in 70+ cities. The previous site was generic and did not link to real events, so it was hard to tell whether the project was still active.

  • Faster: a lighter page that loads quickly, also on mobile
  • Events on the homepage: upcoming events are visible straight away
  • Find your city: pick a city and see what is on there
  • Clear activities: language exchange, dinners, photowalks, board games and city games each get a short description
  • Easy contacts: a clear way to say hello or start a group in your city
  • Story and numbers: since 2017, 70+ cities, 150+ volunteers, so it looks alive

Open the new site.
